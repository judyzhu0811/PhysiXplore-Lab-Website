OUR STORY

Making physics something every student can explore.

PhysiXplore Lab began with a simple idea: physics should not be something students have to wait until high school to discover.

Through hands-on experiments, outreach events, and educational resources, we are working to make physics more accessible, engaging, and approachable for students everywhere.

What started as a small effort to introduce students to physics has grown into a student-led initiative focused on bringing meaningful STEM experiences to communities that may not otherwise have access to them.
