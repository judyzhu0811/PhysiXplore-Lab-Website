FREE RESOURCES

Explore. Experiment. Discover.

Download free resources to help you explore physics through hands-on experiments and scientific discovery. We’re making our all our printable material from the kits publicly available because, as an organization founded by students from nontraditional and homeschool backgrounds, we believe our curriculum should be accessible to all students. We understand that some classrooms may prefer to purchase materials independently, so we’re sharing exactly what we use, where we source it, and how you can assemble the kits yourself if you wish. For most classrooms with fewer than 50 students, purchasing the materials directly through us will be more cost-effective. However, we recognize that every classroom has different needs and preferences, and we want teachers to have the flexibility to choose whichever option works best for them. Do not monetize any of the following resources without our permission. Please reach out to physixplorelab@gmail.com if you have any questions about this policy.
