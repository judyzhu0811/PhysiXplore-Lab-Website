Electricity & Magnetism Workshop

Saturday, October 24 · Atlas Church

Join STEAM Labs on October 24th from 3:00-5:00 pm at Atlas Church filled with fun, hands-on physics workshops, led by experienced high school students passionate about STEM! In this workshop, we will have live demonstrations and students will be able to explore the properties of electricity and magnetism by building their own electric motor. Through these activities, students will discover how electromagnetic induction makes motors work. Our goal is to foster curiosity among younger students in STEM subjects while building a solid foundation for future science subjects. All materials will be provided for free, and students will be able to take their projects home!

For Students Grades 3 & Up

STEAMLabs: A Hands-on Introduction to Physics

Monday-Friday, August 3-8 · After School Learning Tree

STEAMLabs Introduction to Physics Course (12:00-2:00 pm): An introductory hands-on physics class taught by high school students accepted to highly selective summer programs, intended to introduce younger students to the joy of learning physics. We will cover fundamental concepts ranging from mechanics to electricity and magnetism to waves and light through engaging lessons, live demonstrations, and take-home crafts. Our goal is to foster curiosity and help students view physics not as a burden, but as an exciting and accessible subject while building a strong foundation for future physics classes.

For students in grades 3–6

A student-led initiative turning physics into applicable experiments that people of all ages can understand!
